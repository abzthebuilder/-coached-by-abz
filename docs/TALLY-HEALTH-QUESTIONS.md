# Health questions for the Tally application form

Add these to your Online Coaching application form so you know about anything that needs a GP's OK
before you write someone's plan. They're based on the standard pre-exercise screening most gyms use.

## Questions (Yes / No)

1. Has a doctor ever said you have a heart condition, or that you should only do exercise a doctor recommends?
2. Do you get chest pain, either at rest or when you exercise?
3. Have you ever lost your balance because of dizziness, or lost consciousness?
4. Do you have high or low blood pressure, diabetes, asthma or another medical condition?
5. Do you have a bone, joint or muscle problem, or a current injury, that could get worse with exercise?
6. Are you taking any medication that could affect exercise (for example for blood pressure or your heart)?
7. Are you pregnant, or have you had a baby in the last six months?
8. Do you have, or have you had, an eating disorder?
9. Is there any other reason you think you shouldn't do physical activity?

Then add a long-answer question:

> If you answered yes to any of these, tell me a bit more:

and a required tick box:

> I confirm I'm 18 or over, my answers are true to the best of my knowledge, and I'll tell ABZ about any
> changes to my health. I've read the terms and health disclaimer at https://YOUR-SITE/terms and the
> privacy policy at https://YOUR-SITE/privacy.

## What to do with the answers

If someone answers **yes** to any of 1 to 9, ask them to check with their GP that it's OK to start a new
training and nutrition plan **before** you write it, and keep a note that you asked.
